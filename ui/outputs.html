Emails and PDFs

Everything Cleanpost sends or generates outside the app. Emails never contain PHI: they carry counts, client names and links into the app. PDFs that do contain PHI (the posting worksheet) say so in the footer. Outputs always render in the light theme.

EmailMVPTemplate e-05
Purpose
Reach the right person immediately for problems that cannot wait for the digest.
Data
Alert type, client, what stopped, since when. Types: connection failed; out-of-balance remittance over the set amount; timely filing within 3 days; priority 1 item past SLA.
Actions
One primary link to the record (e.g. Reconnect opens I-04).
States
Repeats at most every 4 hours per alert while unresolved; respects quiet hours except timely filing.
Rules
No PHI. Subject names the client, never a patient.
