Product

Everything between the charge and the posted payment.

Cleanpost reads coded charges from each client's PM, checks and sends claims, tracks them, and posts or verifies every payment. Billers see only what needs a person.

Claims out

Every claim checked before it goes out.

  • Eligibility checked on new or changed coverage.
  • Payer rules, NCCI edits, modifiers and diagnosis pointers checked on every claim.
  • A confidence score decides what goes out automatically. You set the threshold per client and payer.
  • Fixes are suggested with the evidence behind them. Billers accept, edit or dismiss.

Needs reviewMode A
Nguyen, M. · Oct 27, 2026 · UnitedHealthcare · $312.00
Confidence72 · threshold 90
Suggested

Add modifier 25 to line 1. UnitedHealthcare requires it when a visit and a minor procedure are billed the same day.

Payments in

Every remittance balanced to the cent.

  • 835s matched to claims and lines.
  • Adjustments mapped to each PM's own codes.
  • Posted into the PM, or checked line by line against what the PM posted.
  • Denials written off by mistake are caught and reopened.

How it works

Nothing changes in the practice.

Cleanpost connects to each client's PM with a dedicated login. Each client runs in one of three modes, chosen during onboarding and changeable later.

Day 1Connect the PM

API, file exchange or guided manual steps, depending on the PM.

Day 1Dry run

Pull the last 7 days and check the counts and a sample of encounters together.

Weeks 1 to 2Review everything

Every claim goes to a biller first, so you see how Cleanpost scores your claims.

After week 2Raise the threshold

Claims above your confidence threshold go out on their own.

Three ways claims and payments can flow

Most clients start in Mode A because nothing has to be re-enrolled with payers.

Mode A

The PM sends; Cleanpost checks

Use when the PM already submits claims reliably.

Security and HIPAA

How Cleanpost protects your clients' patient data.

Cleanpost is a business associate of each billing company. These are the controls in place, stated plainly.

Business associate agreement

Signed with every customer. Every subprocessor that handles PHI signs one with us.

Encryption

TLS 1.2 or higher in transit. Encrypted databases and storage, with field-level encryption for tax IDs and member IDs.

Access

Two-factor sign-in for every user. Roles and per-client access. Sessions time out after 15 minutes idle.

Audit log

Every view and change of PHI is logged with who, what and when, and kept for six years.

Tenant isolation

Each billing company's data is isolated at the database level and tested in every release.

Minimum necessary

Lists show reduced identifiers. Full member IDs and dates of birth appear only when someone opens them, and that is logged.
